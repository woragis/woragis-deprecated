# Job Applications Domain - Interview Stages

## Overview
How interview stages are managed for job applications.

## Key Points

### Interview Stage Structure
- Interview stages subdomain within jobapplications
- Stages linked to job applications
- Track interview progression (phone screen, technical, final, etc.)
- Status tracking: scheduled, completed, cancelled, no-show

### Stage Management
- Create interview stages for applications
- Update stage details (date, time, location, notes)
- Track stage completion
- Mark stages as cancelled or no-show

### Stage Types
- Phone Screen
- Technical Interview
- Behavioral Interview
- Final Interview
- On-site Interview
- (Customizable per application)

### Integration with Applications
- Stages linked to job_application_id
- Used in resume metrics calculation
- Completed interviews counted for resume statistics
- Interview progression tracked per application
