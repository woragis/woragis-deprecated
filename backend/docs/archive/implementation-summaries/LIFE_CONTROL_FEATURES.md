# Life Control Dashboard - Feature Suggestions

Based on your existing backend architecture, here are cool features that would transform Woragis into a comprehensive personal life control center - not just to show, but to actually control everything in your life.

## 🏠 Smart Home & IoT Control

### Home Automation Hub
- **Smart Device Integration**
  - Connect to Home Assistant, Google Home, Alexa, or Apple HomeKit
  - Control lights, thermostats, locks, cameras from your dashboard
  - Create automation rules (e.g., "When I'm in a meeting, dim lights and silence notifications")
  - Energy consumption tracking and optimization suggestions

- **Environment Monitoring**
  - Air quality sensors (temperature, humidity, CO2, air quality index)
  - Weather integration with alerts for outdoor activities
  - Smart scheduling for HVAC based on your calendar and preferences

### Security & Access Control
- **Smart Lock Management**
  - Remote lock/unlock capabilities
  - Guest access codes with time-based expiration
  - Entry/exit logs and notifications
  - Integration with your calendar (auto-unlock when you're expected home)

- **Camera & Surveillance**
  - Live camera feeds in dashboard
  - Motion detection alerts
  - Face recognition for family members
  - Cloud storage management for recordings

## 💰 Financial Control & Automation

### Enhanced Financial Management (Building on existing finances domain)
- **Automated Bill Payments**
  - Connect to bank accounts via Plaid/Yodlee
  - Auto-pay scheduling with approval workflows
  - Bill reminders and payment tracking
  - Subscription management (track all recurring charges, cancel unused ones)

- **Investment Portfolio Control**
  - Real-time portfolio tracking (stocks, crypto, bonds)
  - Automated rebalancing suggestions
  - Tax-loss harvesting alerts
  - Dividend tracking and reinvestment automation

- **Expense Automation**
  - Receipt scanning and automatic categorization
  - Budget alerts and spending limits
  - Cashback optimization (suggest which card to use)
  - Expense approval workflows for shared accounts

### Financial Intelligence
- **Spending Insights**
  - AI-powered spending pattern analysis
  - Predictive cash flow modeling
  - "Can I afford this?" quick checks
  - Financial goal tracking (savings, debt payoff, investments)

### Wellness Automation
- **Smart Nutrition**

  - Integration with food delivery services (auto-order healthy meals)

- **Sleep Optimization**

## 📅 Calendar & Time Control

### Intelligent Scheduling (Building on existing scheduler domain)
- **Smart Calendar Management**
  - Auto-scheduling based on priorities and energy levels
  - Meeting optimization (suggest best times, avoid conflicts)
  - Travel time calculation and buffer insertion
  - Focus time blocking (auto-block time for deep work)

- **Time Tracking & Analysis**
  - Automatic time tracking from calendar
  - Time spent analysis by category (work, personal, learning)
  - Productivity insights (when are you most productive?)
  - Time budget allocation (e.g., max 4 hours meetings per day)

### Life Automation
- **Context-Aware Automation**
  - "Focus mode" that silences notifications, dims lights, plays focus music
  - "Meeting mode" that sets status, blocks calendar, adjusts environment
  - "Sleep mode" that dims lights, sets thermostat, enables Do Not Disturb
  - Location-based automation (when you arrive home, when you leave)

## 🤖 AI-Powered Life Assistant

### Personal AI Agent (Building on existing AI service)
- **Life Decision Support**
  - "Should I take this job?" - analyzes salary, commute, growth potential
  - "What should I do this weekend?" - suggests based on weather, budget, goals
  - "Is this purchase worth it?" - financial impact analysis
  - "How can I optimize my day?" - schedule and energy optimization

- **Predictive Insights**
  - Predict when you'll need to restock groceries
  - Forecast when you'll achieve financial goals
  - Suggest optimal times for important decisions

### Automation Rules Engine
- **If-This-Then-That (IFTTT) Style Automation**
  - "If I'm in a meeting → silence phone, set Slack status, dim lights"

  - "If I haven't exercised in 3 days → suggest workout, block calendar time"

## Technical Implementation Notes

### New Backend Domains Needed
- `smartdevices/` - IoT device management
- `health/` - Health metrics and fitness tracking
- `habits/` - Habit tracking and automation
- `automation/` - Rules engine for life automation
- `integrations/` - External service integrations
- `notifications/` - Smart notification system
- `analytics/` - Life analytics and insights

### API Integrations to Consider
- **Home Automation**: Home Assistant API, HomeKit, SmartThings
- **Health**: Apple HealthKit, Google Fit, Fitbit API, Strava API
- **Finance**: Plaid, Yodlee, Stripe, Coinbase API
- **Calendar**: Google Calendar API, Microsoft Graph API
- **Communication**: Slack API, WhatsApp Business API, Telegram Bot API
- **IoT**: MQTT broker, WebSocket for real-time device control

### Frontend Components Needed
- Real-time dashboard with WebSocket updates
- Device control panels
- Automation rule builder (visual flow editor)
- Analytics visualizations (charts, graphs, trends)
- Quick action buttons
- Notification center
- Settings and integration management
